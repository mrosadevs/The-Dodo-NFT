🗓️ When is mint date for the Dodo? +
Mint date will be announced in our Discord first — join the Den to get early access and whitelist opportunities before the public announcement! Follow us on Twitter for real-time updates too.
📋 How can I join the whitelist for the Dodo? +
Join our Discord community and participate actively! Whitelist spots are rewarded to engaged community members. Male Dodo holders also automatically receive whitelist access for the Female drop at 20% roadmap.
🛒 How can I purchase a Dodo? +
Mint directly from our website when the mint goes live, or purchase from secondary markets like OpenSea after the initial sale. Make sure you have a Web3 wallet with some ETH ready to go!
💰 What is the price of a Dodo? +
Mint price will be announced closer to the mint date in our Discord. Whitelist members will receive a discounted price as a reward for being early supporters. Stay in the Den to be first to know!
⚡ How do I get access to the presale? +
Presale access is exclusively for whitelist members. Earn your spot by being an active, positive member of our Discord, participating in community events, and engaging with us on Twitter!
